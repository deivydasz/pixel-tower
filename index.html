    How to play

    Your tower stands in the middle and shoots the nearest enemy by itself. Enemies come from every side, in waves that keep getting stronger.

    Destroyed enemies burst into pixels. Spend pixels on the upgrades under the game. You start with Damage and Tower HP; Fire rate, Range, Dmg sponge (blocks damage per hit) and Pixel magnet (more pixels per kill) unlock as you reach new waves.

    Tap the BLAST button (or press Space) to blast every enemy on screen. It takes a while to recharge, so save it for emergencies.

    Every 20 waves a boss arrives (every 10 from wave 100). Destroy it to earn a boss core and bonus chips. Wardens are shielded: a Blast breaks the shield for a few seconds.

    Boss cores and chips pay for Bit Theory: timed research that unlocks extra bullets, piercing shots, critical hits, a faster Blast, stacking damage and faster fights. Research keeps running while the game is closed.

    Each cleared wave also gives pixel chips. Chips stay between runs: spend them in the Foundry for permanent boosts. Completing wave 10, 20, 30... for the first time earns a certificate with bonus chips.

    Pause with the Pause button, P or Esc.

    Bit Theory

    Study the pixels to find their deeper meaning. Research runs in real time, even when the game is closed. One study at a time.

    Certificates

    Complete wave 10, 20, 30... for the first time to earn a certificate and a one-time chip reward. Boss waves count once the boss is beaten.

    History

    Highlighted rows set a new best wave.

    Pixel chips come from clearing waves: 1 per wave, plus 1 more for every 5 waves reached. Spend them here on permanent boosts that carry into every future run.
